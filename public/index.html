    ⚙️ How it works — hybrid inference

    1. On-device first: when your browser supports it (Chrome on desktop, Gemini Nano), the message is analysed right on your device. It never leaves your phone or computer — private and free.
    2. Cloud fallback: if no on-device model is available, the same check runs on cloud Gemini through Firebase AI Logic. You always get an answer.
    3. Protected: Firebase App Check guards the AI service, and every check is saved (anonymously) to Firestore so you can review your history.

    Built for the #BuildHybridAI 2026 hackathon (Firebase / Google) with Firebase AI Logic, App Check, Anonymous Auth and Firestore. Demo mode uses a local keyword heuristic — no AI call — until a real Firebase config is added.
